# My Favorite Japan Series: Alice in Borderland

**Alice in Borderland** is my favorite Japanese series. It’s a thrilling *sci-fi survival drama* that keeps you on edge every episode. I love how the game so intense that you couldn't predict what would happened next. 

## Reasons why I love it 
- Very intense and unpredictable story  
- Characters were so fine as wine. They were so beautiful especially to my arisu and chishiya.
- Has great cinematography and visuals. Masterfully crafted, filled with twists and surprises that keep you on the    edge and always yearning for more. 
- This series really pulls you into its enigmatic and deadly game. That's why i love this shit.
